# Live Meeting Assistant — Deferred

Document options, never auto-join meetings without approval. Separate local PC screen/system-audio capture (such as Call.md) from a meeting participant bot and Teams chat bot. The official VideoDB live capture skill may provide live perception but should not be assumed to join or speak in Teams. For any live action, require participant notice, tenant permissions, model/data policies and user-approved tool capabilities. Do not implement for Windows MVP.
