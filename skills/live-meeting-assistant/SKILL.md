# Live Meeting Assistant — Later Phase

Document options for desktop screen/audio capture, Teams meeting participant bots and Teams chat agents separately. VideoDB capture and local video analysis tools do not automatically join, speak or share video in Teams. Require meeting consent, organizational permissions and an explicit list of permitted agent actions. No live Teams integration in MVP.
